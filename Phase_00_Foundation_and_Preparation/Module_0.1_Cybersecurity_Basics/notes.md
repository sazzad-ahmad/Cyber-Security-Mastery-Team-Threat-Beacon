## 📌 Detailed Explanation

### 1. InfoSec vs Cyber Security
- **InfoSec:** Protection of all forms of information (paper or digital).
- **Cyber Security:** Protection of digital assets, networks, and electronic devices.

### 2. CIA Triad
- **Confidentiality:** Ensures data is accessible only to authorized users (Encryption, Access Control).
- **Integrity:** Ensures data remains accurate and unaltered (Hashing - SHA256).
- **Availability:** Ensures services and data are reachable when needed (Backups, DDoS Protection).

### 3. Hacker Types
- **White Hat:** Ethical hackers with written permission working to secure systems.
- **Black Hat:** Malicious hackers operating illegally.
- **Gray Hat:** Hackers operating without authorization but without explicit malicious intent.
